Snapshot is a governance platform. Communities create spaces, write proposals and vote on them. Offchain proposals and votes are messages you sign with your wallet and cost no gas. Onchain actions are transactions on the networks Snapshot supports.

Each space is run by its own community, whose admins choose its settings, such as who can propose and vote and how voting power is counted. We don't control spaces, proposals or their results, and we don't verify them. Whether a result is executed is up to each space and its setup.
