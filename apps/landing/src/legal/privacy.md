This privacy policy explains how Snapshot Labs AG ("we", "us") processes personal data when you use Snapshot, including snapshot.box, its apps and APIs, and the Snapshot MCP server, or when you contact us. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

## Who we are

Snapshot Labs AG, Dorfstrasse 38, 6340 Baar, Switzerland, is responsible for the processing described here. For any question about your data, including your rights, write to admin@snapshot.org.

## What we collect

### When you visit Snapshot

Technical data your browser sends, such as your IP address, browser and operating system, the page you came from, and the time of your request. We use it to run and secure Snapshot.

### When you use Snapshot

Your wallet address and ENS name, the profile details you add, such as a name, avatar or description, the spaces you create or manage and your roles in them, your proposals, votes, follows and delegations, and your voting power, which is calculated from public blockchain data. This data is public (see "Public and permanent data" below).

### When you use Snapshot through an AI assistant

When you connect an AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to the Snapshot MCP server (mcp.snapshot.box), we process:

- your wallet address, to link the assistant to your account
- a signer wallet ("alias") created for you when you authorize the connection. Its key is held by our provider Coinbase Developer Platform, which therefore receives the content of each message it signs, such as a vote and its reason or the text of a proposal. It can only sign messages on Snapshot, such as votes, proposals and follows, and can't send transactions or move funds. You can revoke it at any time in your settings.
- the requests your assistant sends to the server, such as queries, votes and proposals. Votes, proposals and follows are published like any other action on Snapshot.
- technical logs for each request: a request identifier, the action, a shortened wallet address, and error details when a request fails

The server only receives your assistant's requests, not your conversations with it. Your assistant's provider processes those under its own privacy policy, and Coinbase processes data under [its privacy policy](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/privacy).

### When you subscribe to emails

If you subscribe to email notifications, we use your email address to send them. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each email, or by writing to us.

### When you contact us

When you write to us, by email or on platforms such as Discord, GitHub or X, we process your message and contact details to reply. Those platforms process your data under their own privacy policies.

## Why we process it

- To provide Snapshot and the features you use (performance of a contract).
- To run, secure and improve Snapshot, and to prevent abuse (our legitimate interest).
- To send the emails you subscribed to (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
- To comply with the law, and to establish or defend legal claims (legal obligation and legitimate interest).

## Public and permanent data

Spaces, proposals, votes, follows, delegations, profiles and the wallet addresses behind them are public. We store them in our database and publish them to IPFS, and onchain actions are recorded on blockchains. Anyone can read and copy this data, and once it is on IPFS or a blockchain, neither we nor anyone else can change or delete it. Some of your rights, such as deletion, therefore can't fully apply to it. If that isn't acceptable to you, don't publish data on Snapshot. You are responsible for what you publish, and we are not liable for data you publish to IPFS.

## Who we share it with

- Service providers that help us run Snapshot, such as hosting, infrastructure, IPFS and blockchain node providers, and Coinbase Developer Platform for the Snapshot MCP server. They process data on our behalf.
- Professional advisers, such as lawyers and auditors, when needed.
- Authorities in Switzerland or abroad, when the law requires or allows it, or to protect our rights.
- A buyer or partner, if we sell, merge or reorganize all or part of our business.

## Transfers abroad

Our providers may process data outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area, including in countries whose laws don't protect personal data as well. In that case we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, adapted to Swiss law where needed, or on an exception the law allows, such as your consent or the performance of a contract.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, to meet legal retention duties, or to defend legal claims. Then we delete or anonymize it. Data on IPFS and blockchains can't be deleted (see "Public and permanent data").

## Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no system is fully secure. If a data breach happens, we notify you and the authorities when the law requires it.

## Your rights

Under applicable law, you can ask to access your data, correct or delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw your consent at any time, for the future. To do so, write to admin@snapshot.org. These rights have legal limits, and using some of them may stop us from providing Snapshot to you. You can also complain to a data protection authority, which in Switzerland is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest change, and changes apply once published here.
